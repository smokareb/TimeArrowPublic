Privacy Policy — Time Arrow

Last updated: 17 August 2026  ·  Applies to the Android app com.timearrow.game

Time Arrow has no accounts and no sign-in, and your game progress never leaves your device. The game does show optional rewarded video ads, supplied by Google AdMob. When you choose to watch one, Google receives your device's advertising identifier so it can select and measure that ad. Nothing else is collected, and we never receive any personal information about you ourselves.

1. Who this is from

Time Arrow is developed and published by Salamander Man. For any question about this policy, or about the app, write to s.mokareb@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We — the developer — collect nothing. We operate no server, receive no data about you, and have no analytics. We cannot identify you, contact you, or see anything you do in the game.

Our ad provider collects some data, but only when you opt in to an ad. Rewarded video ads are optional and never play by themselves: they appear only after you tap the "Watch an ad for Shards" button in the in-game shop. When you tap it, Google AdMob loads an ad and, in doing so, processes:

  • your device's advertising identifier (a resettable ID you control in Android Settings);
  • coarse technical information about your device and app, such as model, operating system version, language and approximate region derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction events — that an ad was requested, shown, viewed or clicked.

Google uses this to select, cap, measure and prevent fraud on ads, and — where you have permitted it — to personalise them. Google acts as an independent controller for that processing. Their handling is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the AdMob privacy documentation.

If you never tap the ad button, no ad is ever requested and none of the above takes place.

3. Information stored on your device

So that you don't lose your progress between sessions, the game writes a single record to your device's local app storage. It never leaves your phone.

What is storedWhy
Which levels you have unlocked and how many stars you earnedTo resume where you left off
Your best arrow count per levelPersonal best tracking
Arcade records (best Zen streak, best wave, best score)Personal best tracking
Shards, purchased upgrades, and the arrow trail / bow accent you equippedTo keep what you unlocked
Your sound, music, volume and haptics settingsTo keep your preferences

This record contains nothing that identifies you. It is stored under the key timeArrow.v4 in the app's private storage area, which no other app on your device can read.

How to delete it

  • In the game: Settings → Reset progress clears level progress, Shards and unlocks.
  • On your device: Settings → Apps → Time Arrow → Storage → Clear data erases everything.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

5. Advertising

Time Arrow shows rewarded video ads only. There are no banners, no interstitials between levels, and no ad that plays without you asking for it. The only place an ad can start is the "Watch an ad for Shards" button in the shop. Declining costs you nothing: the entire game, all 80 levels and both arcade modes, is completable without ever watching one.

Ads are supplied by Google AdMob. See section 2 for what they process.

How to limit ad personalisation

  • Android Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you delete or reset your advertising ID, which breaks the link to any past activity.
  • The same screen has an opt out of ads personalisation switch. You will still see ads, but they will not be tailored to you.
  • Where required by law, a consent prompt appears the first time an ad is requested, and your choice is respected on every later request.

6. Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Shards are an in-game score that can only be earned by playing. They have no monetary value, cannot be bought, and cannot be transferred or redeemed.

7. Children

Time Arrow is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that raises a concern, contact us at the address in section 12.

8. Third parties

The app includes exactly one third-party SDK: Google AdMob, for optional rewarded video ads. There are no analytics providers, no social logins, no cloud save service and no other SDK of any kind.

We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising in the sense used by the CCPA/CPRA. Serving a personalised ad may nonetheless count as "sharing" under some readings of that law; resetting your advertising ID or switching off ads personalisation in Android Settings is the effective opt-out, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals where the ad SDK surfaces them.

9. Data security

Your saved game never leaves your device and lives in the app's private storage, protected by Android's normal per-app sandboxing. Ad requests are made over an encrypted HTTPS connection by the Google AdMob SDK. We hold no server and no copy of anything.

10. Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, portability and erasure under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none — the only data that exists on our side is nothing at all, and the save file on your own device, which you can inspect, reset or delete at any time using the steps in section 3.

For data processed by Google in connection with ads, exercise your rights directly with Google using the controls in Google My Ad Center and the advertising-ID controls in Android Settings. You can also write to us at the address in section 12 and we will point you to the right place.

11. Changes to this policy
