Privacy Policy — Time Arrow

Last updated: 17 August 2026  ·  Applies to the Android app com.timearrow.game

Time Arrow does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. It works entirely offline. Everything it saves stays on your device.

1. Who this is from

Time Arrow is developed and published by Salamander Man. For any question about this policy, or about the app, write to s.mokareb@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

None. The app collects no personal information, no device identifiers, no advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no usage analytics and no crash telemetry. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, because the app makes no network requests at all.

3. Information stored on your device

So that you don't lose your progress between sessions, the game writes a single record to your device's local app storage. It never leaves your phone.

What is storedWhy
Which levels you have unlocked and how many stars you earnedTo resume where you left off
Your best arrow count per levelPersonal best tracking
Arcade records (best Zen streak, best wave, best score)Personal best tracking
Shards, purchased upgrades, and the arrow trail / bow accent you equippedTo keep what you unlocked
Your sound, music, volume and haptics settingsTo keep your preferences

This record contains nothing that identifies you. It is stored under the key timeArrow.v4 in the app's private storage area, which no other app on your device can read.

How to delete it

  • In the game: Settings → Reset progress clears level progress, Shards and unlocks.
  • On your device: Settings → Apps → Time Arrow → Storage → Clear data erases everything.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

4. Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhat it is used for
VIBRATE Short haptic feedback when you fire an arrow, hit a target or take damage. You can turn this off in Settings.
INTERNET Declared by the app framework as standard. The current version makes no network requests of any kind; the game is fully playable in aeroplane mode.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, or any other sensitive permission.

5. Advertising

This version of Time Arrow contains no advertising and no advertising SDK. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, the Play Store listing will show the "Contains ads" label, and the Data safety section of the listing will be updated to describe exactly what the ad provider collects.

6. Purchases

There are no in-app purchases. Shards are an in-game score that can only be earned by playing. They have no monetary value, cannot be bought, and cannot be transferred or redeemed.

7. Children

Time Arrow is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. Because the app collects no data at all, it knowingly collects no data from children.

8. Third parties

The app includes no third-party SDKs, no analytics providers, no ad networks, no social logins and no cloud save service. No data is shared with anyone, because none is gathered.

9. Data security

Since no data leaves your device, there is no transmission to secure and no server-side copy to breach. Your saved game lives in the app's private storage, protected by Android's normal per-app sandboxing.

10. Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, portability and erasure under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none. The only data that exists is the save file on your own device, which you can inspect, reset or delete at any time using the steps in section 3.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page changes with it, and any change that expands what the app collects will be published here before the version that makes the change is released.
